Concept · wallet-native perps

Perps inside the wallet people already use.

A wallet adds one tag and its users get a full perps app: Strike's markets, charts, leverage, auto close (take profit / stop loss) and history, all trading on Strike. The wallet earns a builder fee on every fill through Strike's builder program.

Tap Perps in the phone to try it on Strike's testnet.

Three ways to try it

Pick by how much time you have. Testnet is the default everywhere; add ?network=mainnet to any demo link for mainnet.

No wallet2 minutes

Look around

  1. Open Halo, a wallet with Strike perps built in: live prices, order book and charts from Strike.
  2. Try the other two hosts: Kairo (a DEX) and Tessera (a portfolio tracker).
  3. Open the mainnet dashboard for StrikeOn to see real fills and builder fees.
  4. Check the transactions below on Cardanoscan.
Open HaloSee the proof
Preprod wallet10 minutes

Trade on testnet

  1. Switch your Cardano wallet (we tested Eternl and Lace) to Preprod and get test ADA from the Cardano faucet.
  2. Open Halo on ETH-USD and click Connect wallet: one signature, no funds move.
  3. Deposit about 500 tADA (a new Strike account's first deposit must be at least $100). Credit takes a few minutes.
  4. Open and close a small ETH trade, try TP/SL, a limit order, then Withdraw.
Open Halo on testnet
Your builder code5 minutes

Plug in your own code

  1. Open the developers page and type your builder code; pick Testnet or Mainnet.
  2. Trade in the live preview: with your code it reports like a real integration.
  3. Click Copy snippet to get the one-tag install for any site.
  4. Click Open your dashboard, then Sync fills, to see your trades and builder fees.

Good to know while testing

  • Our demos use our builder codes. A ?builder= in a demo link is ignored, so nobody can route a demo's fees to another code. Use the developers page for your own code.
  • Deposits are credited by Strike after Cardano confirmations, usually a few minutes.
  • Withdrawals cost Strike's $1 fee plus a small funding transaction (about 2 ADA) that Strike asks for on every Cardano payout.
  • A builder can't trade through its own code (Strike refuses it). Connect a different wallet from the one that registered the code.
  • Safety: the widget never holds funds; every deposit and withdrawal is signed in your wallet after our checks. Details in SECURITY.md.

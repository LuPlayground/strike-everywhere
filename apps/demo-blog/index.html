Perps come home: trading Strike without leaving the page you're reading

Demo article · 4 min read

Most readers of a newsletter like this one hold ADA. Until now, acting on a market view meant opening another site, connecting a wallet again and learning a new interface. The box beside this article changes that: it is Strike's order book, embedded here with a single HTML tag.

You connect the Cardano wallet you already use. Strike creates your account with one signature, you deposit ADA and you trade. Funds sit in your Strike account, not with this site; the trading key the widget creates cannot withdraw anywhere but back to your own wallet.

Why a publication would embed an exchange

Strike runs a builder program. Any site can register a builder code and add a small fee to trades its readers make, credited by Strike on every fill. For a community, that turns attention into a sustainable income stream without ads or paywalls. For Strike, every embedding site becomes a new front door.

"The best trading interface is the one already in front of you."

Risk, plainly

Perpetual futures are leveraged. Prices can move against you quickly and positions can be liquidated. The widget shows an estimated liquidation price and warns you when it is close, but it cannot make leverage safe. Strike is not available in restricted regions; check Strike's terms before trading.

The ticket defaults to modest leverage and shows every fee before you confirm: Strike's own trading fee plus this site's builder fee. Nothing is hidden in the spread.

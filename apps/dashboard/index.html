Volume, fills, active traders and builder fees count only fills in Strike's public fill history for orders the widget registered before Strike created them (same account). Funded accounts and deposit value come from settled deposits in Strike's public transaction history. Builder fees are computed as executed notional × the order's builder-fee bps. Referred users, partner ids and sites are reported by the widget.
